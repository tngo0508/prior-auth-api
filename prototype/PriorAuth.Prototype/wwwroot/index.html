How to read this

  
    
    
  1. Step 1 and 2 are the rule's "which services need prior authorization" and "what documentation is required". Here it is a simple list. The full standards for these are Da Vinci CRD and DTR.
    2. 
    
  2. Step 3 is the request. The standard is Da Vinci PAS. The EHR sends a FHIR Bundle holding a Claim marked preauthorization. The API answers with a ClaimResponse.
    3. 
    
  3. The first answer is almost always pended (code A4), because a person has to decide.
    4. 
    
  4. After the reviewer acts, Step 4 returns approved (A1, with an end date and an authorization number), denied (A3, with a specific reason), or still pended with a note saying what is missing.
